PLEASANTLY UNPREDICTABLE

A little method
to the randomness.

Oddly pairs words you know with animals you might not. Every name is written in PascalCase: no spaces, each word starts with a capital.

  1. CleanOne familiar adjective + one animal. Short and memorable.
  2. CuriousTwo different adjectives + an animal, with a wider vocabulary.
  3. WildAn invented word + an uncommon adjective + an animal. Longer, stranger, and drawn from billions of combinations.

Names are picked with your browser’s cryptographic random generator. The last 2,000 generated names won’t repeat in this session.

Combination counts describe the recipe’s search space, not the number of available accounts. Oddly doesn’t contact platforms or check availability.

A random name can keep personal details out of your handle. Reusing it across platforms can still connect your accounts. It’s a pseudonym, not an anonymity service.

Generation runs on your device. No analytics, remote fonts, or third-party requests from this app. Your hosting provider may still keep standard request logs.
